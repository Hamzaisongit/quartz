
Opinions will be of all sorts. you'll find a different one from every other guy, be it some expert or just any other guy on street.. if you think that you'll get it right by studying all the opinions out there and THEN making the right choice for your self then you'll be playing it smart and sound.. 
this idea sounds wise, but only in theory, i have come to realize!
But lokking at my own experience till now is that it doen't work..
and opposed to this method.. what i have observed instead to work with people whom i admire and aspire to be like someday is this..
- you decide very clearly what you really want, 
- make a plan how you'd achieve it (however stupid or immature it may sound to somebody - though sounding reasonable to you at least), 
- and then giving yourself all in to the plan ***wholeheartedly*** - i personally would like to call it [being serious](https://www.experimental-history.com/p/surely-you-can-be-serious) about what you have decided to have wanted & about following the plan 
- don't change the plan until there's some!
