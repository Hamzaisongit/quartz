 - **Don't think that your actions have inherent virtue, just because you're doing it holistically, or in good faith or in the way paul-graham says :** Say you're learning node, why are you learning it; why are you learning it.. as of now what goes on in mind is this.. i perceive things to be intrinsically virtuous.. like wow i am learning node, i am learning to be able to appreciate the beauty of it, how great how cool, and that if i keep learning like this then somehow *i'll be great, & virtuous and results will come walking to me; just because i am doing it the virtuous way.
	But i have come to realize this very obvious thing which is that without having a clearly known (or at least somewhat clearly known) end goal.. it will not take me to where i want to be.. because the funny thing is i haven't even decided where i want to go, there's only this vague sense of aspiration *to be great, to be virtuous.*

	I always keep looking for ideas and preachings sounding like this,
	- what's the best way to..
	- what's the most correct and sound mental models for..
	and that's a part of this fuss, without really having decided my goal or the end result that i will definitely be satisfied with.. how can any of this best ways, the right ways and the best mental models are going to be fruitful ?
	
	and when you keep acting out of this entitlement, you will keep thinking that you will eventually reach somewhere where great people are.. but that's simply untrue.. you'll be nowhere 
	
	Because all strategies are bound to or subject to what the desired result is 
	i never set realistic goals and interests
- **Not being loyal to things:** instead always being serious about your goals and objectives and being analytically observant of things..
	- always be conscious of the ***point of sale*** and try to be as close as possible to it
